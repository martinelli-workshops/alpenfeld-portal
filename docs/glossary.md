# Glossary

| Term                 | Definition                                                                                                                                       | Avoid                            |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Business Customer    | A business (craft business, industrial workshop, or facility manager) that buys from Alpenfeld under its own Contract Prices.                   | Client, Company                  |
| Key Account          | A Business Customer that a Sales Representative looks after personally.                                                                          |                                  |
| Buyer                | An employee of a Business Customer who searches products and places Orders in the portal.                                                       | Orderer                          |
| Purchasing Manager   | An employee of a Business Customer who approves Orders above an Order Limit and manages the Buyers of the Business Customer.                    | Approver, Admin                  |
| Sales Representative | An Alpenfeld employee who answers Quote Requests and looks after Key Accounts.                                                                   | Sales Rep, Account Manager       |
| Customer Service     | The Alpenfeld team that processes Returns and helps Business Customers with their Orders.                                                       | Support, Helpdesk                |
| Product              | A good Alpenfeld sells, identified by its Article Number.                                                                                    | Item                             |
| Article Number       | The unique number of a Product as defined in the PIM.                                                                                            | SKU, Product Code                |
| Category             | A group of Products in the product hierarchy of the PIM, used for browsing.                                                                     |                                  |
| Product Data         | Description, technical data, Category, and images of a Product, mastered in the PIM.                                                            |                                  |
| List Price           | The public price of a Product, valid for anyone without a Contract Price.                                                                        |                                  |
| Contract Price       | The price of a Product agreed for one Business Customer, mastered in the ERP.                                                                   | Customer Price, Special Price    |
| Stock                | The quantity of a Product available for delivery, mastered in the ERP.                                                                         | Inventory                        |
| Cart                 | The Order Lines a Buyer collects before placing an Order or sending a Quote Request.                                                            | Basket, Shopping Basket          |
| Order Line           | One Product with its quantity and price within a Cart, Order, Quote, or Return.                                                                 | Position, Line Item              |
| Order                | A Buyer's binding request to buy the Order Lines of a Cart at the Contract Prices or the prices of a Quote.                                   | Purchase, Purchase Order         |
| Order Status         | The state of an Order: Pending Approval, Rejected, Placed, or Transferred.                                                                     |                                  |
| Order Limit          | The Order total in CHF up to which a Buyer can place an Order without Approval, set per Buyer by the Purchasing Manager.                      | Budget, Spending Limit           |
| Approval             | The decision of a Purchasing Manager to let an Order above a Buyer's Order Limit proceed or to reject it.                                     | Release, Authorization           |
| Quote Request        | A Buyer's request to a Sales Representative for a lower price for the Order Lines of a Cart.                                                 | RFQ, Inquiry                     |
| Quote                | A Sales Representative's answer to a Quote Request with a price per Order Line and a validity date.                                            | Offer, Quotation                 |
| Return               | A Buyer's request to send back Order Lines of a delivered Order, accepted or rejected by Customer Service.                                     | RMA, Return Request              |
| Return Status        | The state of a Return: Requested, Accepted, or Rejected.                                                                                        |                                  |
| Synchronization      | The hourly copy of Product Data from the PIM and of Contract Prices and Stock from the ERP into the portal.                                   | Import, Sync                     |
| Order Transfer       | The handover of a placed Order to the ERP for processing, exactly once, retried after an ERP outage.                                           | Export, Order Submission         |
| ERP                  | Alpenfeld's enterprise resource planning system; master of Contract Prices, Stock, order processing, and invoices.                             |                                  |
| PIM                  | Alpenfeld's product information management system; master of Product Data and images.                                                         |                                  |
