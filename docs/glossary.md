# Glossary

| Term             | Definition                                                                                                                                 | Avoid                  |
|------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------|
| Business Customer | A business that buys from Alpenfeld Supply through the portal. It owns its Buyers, its Purchasing Manager, and its Contract Prices.       | Client, Company        |
| Buyer            | An employee of a Business Customer who places Orders.                                                                                      |                        |
| Purchasing Manager | An employee of a Business Customer who approves Orders above the Order Limit and manages the Buyers of that Business Customer.           |                        |
| Sales Representative | An Alpenfeld Supply employee who answers Quote Requests and looks after key accounts.                                                    | Sales Rep, Salesperson |
| Customer Service | The Alpenfeld Supply team that processes Returns and helps Business Customers with their Orders.                                           | Support                |
| Product          | A tool, fastener or workshop supply that Alpenfeld Supply sells. Its master data comes from the PIM.                                       | Item, Article, SKU     |
| Contract Price   | The price a Business Customer has agreed for a Product. The ERP is its master.                                                             | Special Price, Net Price |
| Stock            | The quantity of a Product available for delivery, as held in the ERP.                                                                      | Inventory              |
| Order            | A Buyer's request to buy one or more Products at Contract Prices, processed through the ERP.                                               | Purchase Order, Booking |
| Order Limit      | The amount above which an Order needs an Approval by a Purchasing Manager.                                                                 | Threshold, Spending Limit |
| Approval         | A Purchasing Manager's decision to accept or reject an Order above the Order Limit.                                                        | Sign-off, Authorization |
| Quote Request    | A Buyer's request for a price offer for a large quantity of Products, answered by a Sales Representative in the portal.                   | RFQ, Inquiry, Enquiry  |
| Return           | A Buyer's request to send Products back to Alpenfeld Supply, processed by Customer Service.                                                | RMA, Complaint         |
| Portal Copy      | The portal's own copy of Product data, Contract Prices, and Stock, used when the PIM or the ERP is unavailable.                            | Cache, Mirror, Replica |
| Refresh          | The update of the Portal Copy from the PIM and the ERP, performed every hour.                                                              | Sync, Synchronization  |
| Order Transfer   | The transfer of an accepted Order from the portal to the ERP, done exactly once as soon as the ERP is available.                          | Sync                   |
| ERP              | The external system for Contract Prices, Stock, Order processing, and invoices.                                                            |                        |
| PIM              | The external system for Product data and images.                                                                                           |                        |
